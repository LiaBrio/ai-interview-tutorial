Privacy Policy — YouTube Uploader

Last updated: October 19, 2026

YouTube Uploader is a personal automation tool used solely by its owner. This policy explains what data it touches.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload), the tool can upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read subscriptions, comments, watch history, or any other Google account data.

How data is used

Access is used for one purpose only: uploading the owner's self-produced videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored in a private file in the owner's own Google Drive, accessible only to the owner. No tokens or video content are stored on any third-party server.

Data retention and deletion

Tokens remain until the owner revokes access at Google Account permissions, at which point the tool can no longer act. Uploaded videos are managed through YouTube Studio like any other upload.
