YouTube Uploader

YouTube Uploader is a personal automation tool built and used solely by its owner, Labor Lau. It uploads self-produced short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (Labor Lau) through the YouTube Data API v3, on a daily schedule.

It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups, and no data collection from anyone else.

What it does

  • Generates a short vertical video once per day from the owner's own content pipeline.
  • Uploads the video to the owner's YouTube channel via the YouTube Data API, with the owner's explicit OAuth consent (youtube.upload scope).
  • Nothing is published anywhere else, and no third party receives any data.
